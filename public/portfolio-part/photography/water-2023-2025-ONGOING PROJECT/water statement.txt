
While zooming in with my camera on a lake, I noticed something mesmerizing—the reflections on the water became almost unrecognizable. The familiar ripples and waves transformed into abstract patterns, shifting between reality and illusion. At that moment, the surface of the lake was no longer just water; it became something fluid yet unfamiliar, something in between movement and stillness.

The ambiguity of these reflections fascinated me. The way light and form distorted so naturally yet so unpredictably made me want to recapture it, to freeze these fleeting moments before they disappeared. I found myself unable to stop, drawn into the endless variations of patterns, textures, and shapes. This work is my attempt to preserve that feeling—the strange beauty of something as ordinary as water becoming something surreal and otherworldly.
